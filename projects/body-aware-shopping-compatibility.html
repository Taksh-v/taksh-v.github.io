Body-aware shopping compatibility

01

The product idea

The long-term goal is a compatibility layer that considers a shopper’s body structure, preferences, and context, then ranks real garments from a structured catalog. Context could include occasion, location, weather, budget, and style. This notebook explores one early input to that larger system: an image-based body profile.

Intended experience

Help people find existing garments that work for their needs, then explain the reasons behind each recommendation. The aim is to rank real products, not generate new clothing.

Current notebook scope

Estimate landmarks and human-parsing regions from an image, derive provisional body geometry, and assemble a structured profile. It does not retrieve or recommend products.

02

What the notebook does

The inspected notebook, Body_profile.ipynb, uses Colab GPU inference with RTMPose through rtmlib, ONNX Runtime GPU, and the FASHN human parser.

04

What still needs proving

Scale depends on input

Pixel widths are scaled using height entered by the user. Perspective, distance, pose, clothing, crop, and occlusion can change the estimate.

Scores are not probabilities

The saved pose score is 1.004. Its meaning should be checked before using it as confidence; skin-tone confidence is 0.484.

No compatibility engine

The inspected notebook has no product catalog, retrieval, compatibility score, outfit ranking, or shopping links.

No broad validation

One sample run cannot establish measurement accuracy across different people, body types, cameras, lighting, and poses.

05

A credible next sequence

Validate profile estimates first

Use consented, varied images and reference measurements. Track errors and failure cases before describing outputs as measurements.

Build a clean apparel catalog

Represent categories, fit, size information, material, color, price, source, and product links in a consistent schema.

Connect profile and context to ranking

Make profile details correctable by the user, combine them with preferences and occasion, then rank only real catalog products.

Evaluate recommendations with people

Compare against simple baselines and human judgments. Keep fit and body-profile claims inside measured evidence.

Current status: a research prototype.

The notebook demonstrates image analysis and structured profile output. Body measurements are estimates, and the shopping compatibility layer remains future work. The fastest useful review is to inspect the notebook’s masks, geometry calculations, and saved warnings.
