Body-aware shopping compatibility

A Colab prototype exploring how image-derived body structure could inform apparel recommendations alongside garment details and personal context.

01

The problem I’m exploring

Most shopping filters describe a product or an occasion, but rarely connect clothing to a shopper’s body structure and preferences. The larger idea is a compatibility layer that considers a user profile, real garments and context such as occasion, location, weather, budget and style.

Long-term product goal

Recommend real catalog items or complete outfits, then explain why each option fits the user’s request. The system is intended to rank existing products, not generate new clothing.

What this notebook covers

An early computer-vision component: detect pose landmarks, inspect human-parsing masks, derive image-based body geometry and assemble a provisional profile.

02

Prototype pipeline

The inspected notebook is named Body_profile.ipynb. It uses a GPU-backed Colab runtime with RTMPose through rtmlib, ONNX Runtime GPU and the FASHN human parser.

INPUTFull-body imageNotebook sample image and user-entered height/weight.
01 · POSE17 landmarksRTMPose COCO keypoints for shoulders, hips, knees, ankles and more.
02 · PARSEHuman masksFASHN segmentation with torso regions and pose-based arm exclusion.
03 · GEOMETRYWidths + ratiosShoulder, waist and hip widths; torso/leg references; symmetry checks.
OUTPUTProfile V1Provisional shape label, confidence fields and quality warnings as JSON.

The notebook also samples a face region, converts RGB values to CIELAB, filters outliers and estimates a skin-tone category. This is experimental and low-confidence in the saved run.

04

Current limits

Measurements are estimates

Pixel widths are scaled using height supplied by the user. Camera perspective, distance, pose, clothing, cropping and occlusion can shift the result.

Confidence needs checking

The saved pose score is 1.004. Verify score semantics before calling it a probability; the notebook’s skin-tone confidence is 0.484.

No recommendation system yet

The inspected notebook does not include a product catalog, product retrieval, outfit compatibility scoring, ranking or purchase links.

No general validation yet

The visible outputs show one sample run, not measurement error across people, cameras, lighting conditions or body types.

05

Next engineering steps

Validate the profile estimates

Compare against consented reference measurements using consistent photos; record errors and failure cases, not just successful runs.

Build a structured apparel catalog

Represent garment category, fit, cut, material, size information, price, color and product link.

Connect profile and context to ranking

Combine user-correctable profile features with occasion, weather, budget and preferences; rank only real catalog products.

Evaluate recommendation quality

Compare against a simple baseline and human judgments. Keep fit and body-profile claims within measured evidence.

06

Project status

This is a research prototype, not a live product or a validated body-measurement tool. The Colab notebook demonstrates an image-analysis pipeline and a structured body-profile output. The shopping compatibility layer remains the intended next stage.
