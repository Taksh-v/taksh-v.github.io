How I work

Useful beats impressive-looking.

I like making the reasoning inspectable: how data moves, where the system can fail, and what evidence says it works.

Connect the whole path.

Models need retrieval, interfaces, data handling, evaluation, and operational choices around them.

03 / SHOW THE EVIDENCE

Say what is true now.

Ship the useful slice, measure what can be measured, and label prototypes and unknowns clearly.
